About TruthGuard

Understanding the technology behind our Fake News Detection.

How It Works

TruthGuard uses advanced Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Machine Learning techniques to analyze the linguistic patterns of news statements.

📝
Text Input
User submits a news headline or statement.
➔
🧹
Preprocessing
Cleaning, tokenizing, and removing noise.
➔
🔢
TF-IDF Vectorization
Converting text into numerical features.
➔
🤖
ML Prediction
Classification using trained models.

Our Models

During training, we evaluated multiple classification algorithms including Logistic Regression, Multinomial Naive Bayes, Linear SVM, and Passive Aggressive Classifier. The best performing model is deployed here.

Limitations

While our models achieve high accuracy on the training dataset, AI is not perfect. Sarcasm, satire, and highly nuanced context can sometimes lead to incorrect predictions. Always cross-reference important news with verified sources.
